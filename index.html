Privacy Policy

Tile Calculator in Meters — by F-Tech Developers
Last updated: 24 November 2026

Tile Calculator in Meters (the “App”) is developed by F-Tech Developers. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used. The App works fully offline and does not require an account.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal data. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not send any personal data to us.

Data Stored on Your Device

All calculations and calculation history are stored only on your device. Your saved calculations and preferences never leave your device unless you choose to export them: JSON backups are created only when you export them, and only to a location you choose. We do not have access to your calculations, history, or backups.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The only third-party SDK in the App is Google AdMob, which is used to show advertising. AdMob may collect the advertising ID of your device and other device information to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where applicable. This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your device settings (Google Settings > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization) or by resetting your advertising ID.

Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell any data. We do not share personal data with third parties, because the App does not collect any. The only data shared with a third party is the advertising information described above, which is handled directly by Google AdMob on your device.

Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children under the age of 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
